# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- **Pinned apps.** Pin up to five apps to a row above Settings. They stay there when the app is closed; click to open or switch to it. A dot shows which ones are running.
- **Home Assistant.** An HA button beside Settings turns the list into your lights and switches, each with an on/off toggle, plus type-to-search. Your access token is kept in the macOS Keychain.
- **Screenshots.** Two buttons beside Settings: whole screen, or drag to pick an area. Pictures are saved to `~/Pictures/Screenshots` and copied to the clipboard.
- **Choose your buttons.** Settings can show or hide the Home Assistant and screenshot buttons.
- **Three new looks:** Midnight Glass, Vision Air and Neon Edge (six in all, each in dark and light).
- Easier to read text on the highlighted row.
- The app now runs on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## 1.2.0

- Windows on other desktops and in macOS full screen now show in the list, and clicking one jumps straight to it.
- Chrome windows are grouped by profile, with the profile name as a small heading.

## 1.1.0

- Apps with two or more windows list each window under the app. Click a window to switch to it, right-click to put it beside the app you are using, even two windows of the same app.
- Close button on each app (quits it) and on each window (closes just that window), left of minimize.
- The list now grows correctly when windows load while it slides in.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the left-edge app list, apps filling the screen on open, right-click 50/50 split with three split styles, hidden Dock and menu bar, Stage Manager off, Tahoe Glass, Slate Rail and Aurora Capsule looks, dark and light, six accent colours, Start at login.
